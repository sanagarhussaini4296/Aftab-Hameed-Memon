Biography & Academic Progression

Civil Engineering Educator, HEC Approved Ph.D. Supervisor, and Project Management Specialist

PEC Registered Engineer Faculty of Civil & Mechanical Engineering

Prof. Dr. Aftab Hameed Memon serves as Professor in the Department of Civil Engineering at Quaid-e-Awam University of Engineering, Science & Technology (QUEST), Nawabshah, Sindh, Pakistan. He has also served with distinction as the Chairman of the Department of Civil Engineering Technology, where he spearheaded departmental accreditation under the Pakistan Engineering Council Outcome-Based Education (OBE) system, modernized testing facilities, and instituted industry-academia advisory panels.

"My scientific mission focuses on empowering the civil engineering industry with predictive quantitative tools to eliminate cost overruns, diminish construction waste, and foster sustainable infrastructure lifecycle management."

Prior to his academic career at QUEST, Dr. Memon gained valuable engineering industry experience as an Assistant Planning Engineer at Paragon Constructors (Pvt.) Ltd. and Project Coordinator at Osmani and Company (Pvt.) Ltd., where he managed milestone schedules, CPM/PERT networks, progress monitoring, and variation orders on high-value public and commercial schemes.

Research & Scientific Innovations

Core Scientific Thrusts, Competitive National/International Grants, and Invention Honors

Construction Cost & Time Overrun Risk Modeling

Developing empirical risk matrices to identify, isolate, and mitigate budget inflation and schedule slippages in public and commercial mega-projects. Formulated the widely referenced Weighted Risk Assessment Tool for Time and Cost Overrun (WRATTCO).

WRATTCO Tool Delay Analysis Cost Contingency Contract Administration

Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM & AMOS)

Pioneering advanced multivariate structural equation modeling techniques to assess latent human, contractual, and technical variables in civil engineering schemes. Highly cited for standardizing PLS-SEM guidelines across construction management literature.

SmartPLS AMOS Multivariate Statistics Confirmatory Composite Analysis

Sustainable Construction & Circular 3R Waste Systems

Investigating sustainable construction adoption barriers, green building certification (LEED/BREEAM), and physical construction waste minimization strategies. Formulating operational frameworks to curtail on-site material scrap by up to 30%.

Waste Minimization Green Building Life-Cycle Assessment Circular Economy

Lean Construction & Digital Project Delivery

Examining the synergistic integration of Lean Construction practices (Last Planner System, 5S workflow), Building Information Modeling (5D BIM), and AI-driven parametric estimating to enhance construction site safety and labor productivity.

Lean Construction Last Planner 5D BIM AI in Estimating

Contract & Organizational Management

Investigating procurement delivery systems, contract dispute avoidance, claims evaluation under standard conditions of contract (FIDIC / PEC), and organizational behavior factors influencing employee job satisfaction, leadership, and knowledge sharing in construction projects.

Contract Claims Dispute Avoidance FIDIC & PEC Terms Procurement Systems Organizational Behavior

Building Materials & Concrete Technology

Engineering sustainable, high-performance infrastructure materials including low-cost bio-based self-healing concrete using indigenous Pakistani precursors, nylon/steel fiber-reinforced Self-Compacting Concrete (SCC), and supplementary cementitious materials such as Rice Husk Ash (RHA).

Bio-Based Self-Healing Indigenous Materials High-Strength SCC Nylon & Steel Fibers Rice Husk Ash (RHA)

Qualitative & Quantitative Analysis

Applying multi-criteria decision-making (MCDM / TOPSIS), advanced empirical survey instrument formulation, exploratory and confirmatory composite factor modeling, reliability and validity testing, and mixed-methods research paradigms to solve complex civil engineering management problems.

TOPSIS & MCDM Quantitative Modeling Instrument Validation Mixed Methods Decision Support Systems
